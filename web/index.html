    What counts as success?

    For White, the restored rank is R N B Q K B N R. The canonical finish is the 20th move, Qd1#, with the opposing king forced onto the final mating square.

    The published pattern says it works unless the opposing king starts on g3. The black mirror ends in Qd8# and has the analogous g6 restriction.

    Resource logic

    Missing rooks, bishops, or knights can potentially be recreated through pawn promotion. The planner therefore distinguishes “currently present” from “still reconstructable.”
